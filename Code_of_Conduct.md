## Purpose
Our Code of Conduct defines the standards and responsibilities that all members of our Scrum team agree to follow. The purpose is to create a respectful, professional, supportive, and productive environment where everyone can contribute and learn.

## Team Rules
1. Respect Everyone
All team members must treat each other with respect and professionalism. We will respect different opinions, backgrounds, experiences, and viewpoints.
2. Communicate Clearly
Team members should communicate clearly, politely, and honestly. Important information about tasks, meetings, deadlines, or problems should be shared with the whole team.
3. Participate Actively
Every team member is expected to actively participate in team meetings, Scrum activities, labs, studying, and coursework.
4. Complete Agreed Tasks
Each member should make a reasonable effort to complete the tasks they have agreed to undertake within the planned timeframe.
5. Report Problems Early
If a member is unable to complete a task or encounters an obstacle, they should inform the team as soon as possible. Problems should not be hidden or left until the deadline.
6. Support Team Members
Team members should help one another by sharing knowledge, explaining problems, and providing constructive assistance when someone is struggling.
7. Be Honest and Transparent
Members should be honest about their progress, mistakes, and difficulties. Mistakes should be treated as opportunities to learn and improve rather than reasons to blame others.
8. Respect Different Opinions
Everyone should have an opportunity to contribute ideas. Team members should listen to different viewpoints and provide constructive criticism without making personal attacks.
9. Protect Privacy and Confidentiality
Team members must not share another member's private or confidential information without permission. Any project or university information that should remain confidential must be handled appropriately.
10. Act Professionally
All members should behave professionally when communicating with teammates, university staff, and other people involved in the project. We will avoid bullying, harassment, offensive language, discrimination, and other inappropriate behaviour.
Team Commitment
All team members agree to follow this Code of Conduct and to work together to create a positive learning environment. If a problem occurs, we will communicate openly, address the issue professionally, and work together to find a solution.
Our team will focus on collaboration, honesty, respect, continuous improvement, and the successful completion of our project.
